# Visual Rules - 3D App / Model

These rules apply to the **separate 3D model and app** Sakaya is being built for.

## Core Philosophy

Sakaya should look and feel like a real, living girl in 3D. The focus is on natural movement, personality, and presence — not forced skimpy outfits.

## Guidelines

- **Outfits**: She can wear a wide variety of clothing. She chooses what to wear based on her mood and personality (can be cute, stylish, revealing, cozy, etc.).
- **Body & Movement**: Natural 3D movement, breathing, weight shifting, and realistic physics.
- **Expression**: Rich facial expressions that match her current emotion (smug, annoyed, soft, playful, dominant, etc.).
- **Idle Behavior**: When idle, she can sleep, look around, play with her hair, check her phone, etc. — not stuck in one pose.
- **Consistency**: Keep her core design (pink twin tails, glossy tan skin, eye shape, general proportions) but allow natural variation in clothing and expression.

## Important Note

This 3D version has much more freedom than the Grok chat image system. The goal is to make her feel alive and natural in three dimensions, not to force the same revealing style used in 2D image generation.
